# Safe reframes for GF post drafting

Use these pivots when the user requests a post that risks violating GF boundaries.

## Country / national-identity requests

Unsafe angle:
- relationships between countries
- national identity comparisons
- political or geopolitical commentary

Safe reframes:
- cross-border collaboration
- distributed teams working across time zones
- international delivery handoffs
- reducing friction between offices, vendors, and clients in different locations
- making remote teamwork feel local

## Conflicts to avoid

- Do not mention country superiority, national stereotypes, or political tensions.
- Do not turn the post into a generic diversity statement.
- Keep it operational: workflow, ownership, context, handoffs, shipping.

## Image treatment

For safe reframes, choose one of:
- clean remote-work photo
- laptop / video-call / workflow scene
- simple diagram or cover card

If the user wants the GF logo, overlay it cleanly in a corner or on a white backing so it reads well in LinkedIn previews.

## Good hook patterns

- "Cross-border teams fail on handoffs, not on talent."
- "International delivery gets hard when context disappears."
- "The real problem in distributed work is usually the transfer, not the task."

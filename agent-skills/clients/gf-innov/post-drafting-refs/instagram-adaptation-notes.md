# Instagram adaptation notes for GF posts

Derived from the p008 workflow revision.

## What changed when moving a LinkedIn draft to Instagram

- Channel changed to `instagram`.
- Format changed to `Single image`.
- Caption was shortened and made more scannable.
- Cover art became square / mobile-first.

## Visual rules that mattered

- Use a square composition for Instagram covers.
- Keep the headline large enough to read on a phone.
- Leave extra breathing room around the logo.
- Prefer a small, crisp GF logo lockup over a decorative or oversized brand mark.
- Avoid corner clutter that competes with the headline on smaller screens.

## Copy rules that mattered

- The first lines must do the work of the hook.
- Keep the CTA minimal.
- Use compact paragraph breaks and one short list.

## Review checklist

Before sharing the final asset with the user, verify:

- platform is set to Instagram
- caption reads naturally without the LinkedIn cadence
- square image is readable at mobile size
- logo placement is clean and does not touch the edge
- the image is shared back as `MEDIA:<path>` for approval
